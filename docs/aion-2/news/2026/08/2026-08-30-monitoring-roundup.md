# AION 2 Global Monitoring Report — 2026-08-30

## Summary

Initial baseline report for the repository.

The most important current conclusion is that **AION 2 Global still has no officially announced exact release date**. Official material continues to state a 2026 launch / later this year.

The latest important official Global items found in this baseline check are:

1. **2026-08-25 — Pre-release FAQ updated**
2. **2026-08-20 — 30-day Special Quai Membership added to all Founder’s Packs**

---

## 1. Global release status

**Status: CONFIRMED**

- Global launch is planned for **2026**.
- Exact release date is still not officially published in the pre-release FAQ.
- Claims that Global launches in **September 2026** currently exist on third-party sites, but this report does **not** treat them as confirmed.

### Why this matters

Until NCSOFT publishes an exact date, countdowns or specific September dates should be considered speculation unless directly sourced from an official announcement/store listing controlled by NCSOFT.

## 3. Founder’s Pack change

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Status: CONFIRMED / official AION 2 Steam announcement**

NCSOFT added a **30-day Special Quai Membership** to **all Founder’s Packs**, including packs purchased before the change.

### Membership perks currently listed

- Remote Storage
- Personal Trading
- Wind Breeze Merchants
- increased Odyle Energy maximum capacity
- Market access
- Quna Exchange Shop
- increased Odyle Energy Cube selection count in:
  - Expedition
  - Transcendence
  - Sanctuary

This is a meaningful monetization change compared with the originally presented Founder’s Pack contents.

Source:
https://steamcommunity.com/app/3393110/allnews/

## 4. Global business model baseline

**Status: CONFIRMED**

- AION 2 Global will be **free-to-play**.
- Founder’s Packs provide **5 days of early access**.
- Founder’s Packs contain cosmetics / starter benefits depending on tier.
- Special Quai Membership is now included for 30 days in all Founder’s Packs.

### Watch closely

The membership perks include access to systems such as Market, Personal Trading and Quna Exchange Shop. These features are important for evaluating the practical difference between free players and members.

Future reports should track any changes to:

- membership price after the free 30 days;
- Market restrictions;
- personal trading restrictions;
- Quna Exchange rules;
- Odyle Energy limits;
- dungeon / reward-selection advantages;
- regional pricing.
